Maximum Independent Set is NP-hard on graphs excluding the $5\times5$ grid
as an induced minor. A polynomial-time reduction from Max Cut maps a graph
$F$ to a graph $G_F$ in this class such that
$\alpha(G_F)=\operatorname{MaxCut}(F)+2\sum_{uv\in E(F),\,u<v}(v-u)$.
Exclusion of the induced grid minor follows from an obstruction involving
an asteroidal triple of cycles.

This contradicts the polynomial-time conjecture of Dallard–Milanič–Štorgel
and its weighted induced-subgraph generalization unless $\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}$,
and the quasipolynomial-time conjecture of Gartland–Lokshtanov and Korhonen
unless $\mathrm{NP}\subseteq\mathrm{QP}$.
